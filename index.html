Interactive Story Lab for Kids

Story Concepts (Choose Any Adventure)

Several immersive options blending cartoon-style visuals with short-story depth.

1) The Lion King's Moonlight Promise

Simba-like cub crosses glowing savannah trails. Fireflies form memories, while choices unlock courage scenes and friendship endings.

2) Frozen's Northern Lights Quest

Snow spirits dance with aurora ribbons. Kids help Elsa-like heroine shape ice bridges by dragging snowflakes across the screen.

3) Toy Room Time Machine

Toys wake at midnight, assemble a cardboard rocket, and leap through story worlds teaching teamwork, humor, and bravery.

4) Jungle Book River Riddles

Animal friends guide kids through musical riddles. Each right answer animates vines, drums, and dancing silhouettes.

5) The Little Match Girl – Hopeful Reimagining

Warm lantern spirits appear with every match-light moment. Kids build a gentle ending by sharing kindness tokens in interactive scenes.

6) Alice in Wonder Forest

Clock rabbits and talking flowers guide nonlinear chapters. Scroll opens portals; clicks shift perspective and puzzle outcomes.
